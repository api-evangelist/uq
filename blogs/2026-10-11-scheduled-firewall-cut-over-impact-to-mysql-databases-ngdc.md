Scheduled for 2026-10-11 07:00:00 - 2026-10-11 15:00:00 On Sunday the 11th of October 2026, a firewall will be replaced in the Prentice Data Centre. There is an expectation that communication with the data centre will be disrupted for a period of 10 minutes when the physical devices are cut over. As a consequence, there is potential for some centrally managed MySQL databases to become unavailable.
