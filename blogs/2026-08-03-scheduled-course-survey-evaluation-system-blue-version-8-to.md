Scheduled for 2026-08-03 09:00:00 - 2026-08-07 11:59:00 UQs Course Survey Evaluation System (Explorance Blue) will be upgraded from Version 8 to Version 9.7. The current Blue 8 platform is approaching end-of-support and presents increasing operational, security and vendor support risks. This upgrade will move UQ to the vendor-supported Blue 9.7 platform and introduce the new Blue Next architecture while maintaining existing survey administration, reporting and Learn.UQ integrations.
