We are current investigating reports of Guest accounts (non UQ account) in M365 (MS Teams) being prompted for UQ Auth. Update A possible work around is the external signs into m365 via https://teams.cloud.microsoft/ and switch to the UQ tenant if they are using MS Teams for their organisation. (15:24 AEST — Sep 9) Update SharePoint / OneDrive external access may have the same issue.
